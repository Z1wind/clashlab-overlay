# 贡献者公约（简版） / Code of Conduct (short)

## 我们的承诺 / Our pledge

为了营造开放、友好的环境，我们承诺：无论年龄、体型、残障、性别认同、经验、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向，参与本项目都不受骚扰。
We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity.

## 我们的标准 / Our standards

- 使用友好与包容的语言 / Be friendly and inclusive
- 尊重不同观点与经验 / Respect differing viewpoints
- 优雅地接受建设性批评 / Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- 聚焦于对社区最有利的事 / Focus on what is best for the community

## 不可接受的行为 / Unacceptable behaviour

侮辱、人身攻击、公开或私下骚扰、发布他人隐私信息（doxxing）、其他不专业行为。
Insults, personal attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information, or other unprofessional conduct.

## 执行 / Enforcement

违规可向维护者举报；维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本公约的评论、提交、代码与 Issue。
Report violations to the maintainers; they may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this code.
